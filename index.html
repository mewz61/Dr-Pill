    This application safeguards users’ personal and medication data through appropriate security measures. Information is collected solely to facilitate medication reminders and application functionality. Personal data is neither sold nor disclosed without a lawful basis. Users may request access, rectification or erasure, subject to applicable legislation. Access is restricted to authorised personnel.

    Data protection for Medication

    Medication data protection requires the secure collection, storage and processing of sensitive health information. Appropriate safeguards, including encryption, access controls and data minimisation, help prevent unauthorised disclosure. Use information for clearly defined purposes, with transparent privacy practices and respect for individuals’ rights to access, correct, or delete data.
